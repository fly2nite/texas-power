Where the plans come from

Every day this site reads Power to Choose, the providers' own websites, and the facts labels every Texas plan is required to publish, including labels that aren't linked from anywhere. Plans marked "not on Power to Choose" were found that way.

How the price is calculated

Each plan's facts label is turned into its exact pricing formula, which is checked against the label's own published averages before it's used. Your bill is then calculated month by month, and hour by hour for time-of-day plans. When a label can't be fully read, the plan is priced from its published averages and marked as an estimate.

What this doesn't know

Next year won't match last year exactly; a hotter summer costs more on every plan, so the ranking usually holds even when totals move. Delivery charges set by your utility change a few times a year and apply to all plans alike. Always open the facts label before signing up.
